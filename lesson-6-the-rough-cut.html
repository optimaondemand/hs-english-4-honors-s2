A rough version, at full length
Rough means unpolished, not unfinished
●
Rough means unpolished, not unfinished. Every part of your work exists and can be watched, heard, or read from start to end.
Full length for a vlog is three scripted episodes of 6 to 10 minutes each, on camera, in role.
Full length for a podcast is two episodes of 15 to 20 minutes, or one of 30, in role.
Full length for a feature article is 2,500 to 3,500 words, with a headline, an opening summary, pull quotes, images with permission, and sources.
Full length for an imitation is about 3,000 words, or the performed equivalent, following the genre's conventions.
Full length for the Address is a 15 to 20 minute speech in classical shape: an opening, a first turn, the body, a second turn, and a close.
If you have not chosen a medium yet, go back to Lesson 2 before finishing this activity.
