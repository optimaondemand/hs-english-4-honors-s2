Care and Its Limits
Grete’s care, Gregor’s consideration, and the first signs of strain
📖 TODAY’S FOCUS
Part II shows how a household lives with a change, day after day.
Grete takes charge of Gregor’s food and room, and Gregor hides to spare her. The lesson follows both and marks where care begins to narrow.

📚 PART 1 · CONSIDERATION IN A CHANGED BODY

Gregor wakes that evening hungry. A dish of sweet milk with bread, left by his sister, was once his favorite, and now it repels him. The next morning Grete notices the untouched dish and carries it out with a rag. She returns with a spread of foods laid on old newspaper to learn what he will eat: half-rotten vegetables, hard bones, cheese he had called inedible days before, dry bread and bread with butter and salt. Gregor goes straight for the worst of it, and the fresh food he cannot stand. She leaves and turns the key, a signal that he may eat unseen.

His tastes belong to his new body. His thoughts do not. He hides under the couch whenever she enters, because he believes she cannot bear the sight of him. When even that is too much, he spends four hours dragging a sheet over the couch until nothing of him shows. She leaves it in place, and he thinks he sees a look of gratitude.

📚 PART 2 · HOW CARE NARROWS

The story tracks Grete’s care in small steps. At first she enters on tiptoe, as if to a sickroom, and then reports to the parents, who wait outside the door. Within weeks the routine hardens. She comes in, shuts the door behind her at once, runs to the window and throws it open as if short of air, and breathes there before she does anything else. About a month in, she finds Gregor at the window, where he looks most monstrous, and withdraws without entering.

Nothing here is cruelty, and the care continues. Each step adds distance. The narration also notes that Grete has gained a new confidence as Gregor’s interpreter to the parents, so his care has become her task alone. Gregor, for his part, reads her better as time goes on: the longer she pretends there is nothing burdensome in it, the more clearly he sees through the pretense.

Love in this part does not fail through betrayal. It narrows through routine, fatigue, and shared fear.

🔑 KEY TERMS
consideration: attention to the feelings and needs of others.
routine: a fixed series of actions repeated regularly.
distance: in a relationship, the growing space between people who still share a home.
