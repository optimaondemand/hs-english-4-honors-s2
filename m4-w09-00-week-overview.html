Week 9 Overview
The year’s readings are set side by side in one synthesis lesson. The focus is what is owed when a problem belongs to everyone.
📖 THIS WEEK’S READING
No new reading
This week adds no text. The lesson returns to the readings of the year: the seven traditions of the first quarter, Frankenstein and Nietzsche, The Metamorphosis with Emerson, Marcus Aurelius, and the poems, and The Fellowship of the Ring. Each is named on the page, and each can be checked in the course pages or in your own copy.

A year of texts compared: given and made goods, monster and human, the call, and the self alone and among others.
