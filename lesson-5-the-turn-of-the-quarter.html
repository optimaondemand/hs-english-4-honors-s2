Your first third
Finished work, in role, to your audience, not notes
●
The first third is finished work, in role, to your audience. It is not an outline, a script draft, or notes to yourself.
For a vlog, the first third is episode 1: filmed and edited, ready to watch.
For a podcast, the first third is episode 1, or the first 10 minutes, finished and ready to hear.
For a feature article, the first third is the first 1,000 words, with a headline and an opening summary, ready to publish.
For an imitation, the first third is the first third of the piece itself, in the genre's own form, finished.
For the Address, the first third is the opening and the first section of the body, written and rehearsed once on video.
If you have not chosen a medium yet, go back to Lesson 2 before finishing this activity.

Facts and permissions
What your work depends on, and what you may use
●
Your permissions log records every image, sound, map, or quotation you use that is not your own making: a Word template, ITW_PermissionsLog.docx, with one row for each item, naming its source and whether you may use it.
  • Public domain: old enough that no one owns it.
  • Open license: the owner allows use. Name the license.
  • Short quotation for analysis: a few words, credited.
  • Permission granted: you asked and they said yes. Keep the message.
  • Link only: you may point to it, but not copy it.
If your world is Prague or Middle-earth, the novel itself is in copyright: quote no more than a short phrase, reproduce no text or maps, and recreate no scenes.
