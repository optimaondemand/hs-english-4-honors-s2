HOW IT WILL BE READ

Your teacher reads your work first as a member of your audience, then reads the rest as a teacher. Four things are weighed: how well role, audience, goal, and medium fit together (25); how honestly the framework is used and how fairly the other side is stated (30); research and accuracy, including your sources and permissions (25); craft and delivery, meaning care and finish, never equipment (20).
